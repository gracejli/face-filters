Face Hack puts filters on the faces in photos and videos on any website. Everything happens on your computer.

How it works

Turn it on and browse like normal. Face Hack finds the faces in photos and videos on the page and adds whichever filter you picked: dog ears, cat whiskers, sunglasses, a fisheye bulge, big eyes or a tiny face. Click the icon in your toolbar to switch filters, and drag the slider to make the ears bigger or the fisheye stronger. Every face on the page changes as you drag.

Private by design

Face detection runs on your computer with a face model that ships inside the extension. Your photos are never uploaded, there's no account, and nothing is tracked.
