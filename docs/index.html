Blur and block out

How it works

Turn it on and browse like normal. Face Hack finds the faces in photos and videos on the page and adds whichever filter you picked: dog ears, cat whiskers, sunglasses, a guessing-game headband with a picture of your choice, a fisheye bulge, big eyes or a tiny face. Click the icon in your toolbar to switch filters, and drag the slider to make the ears bigger or the fisheye stronger. Every face on the page changes as you drag.

Face blur smudges out faces and necks. Body blur finds whole people, even ones facing away, and blocks them out with a solid color, a pixelated smear, or an image of your choice. Until you upload one, everybody becomes YOUR AD HERE.

Private by design

Face and person detection run on your computer, with models that ship inside the extension. Your photos are never uploaded, any image you add stays in your browser, there's no account, and nothing is tracked.
