Face Hack puts filters on the faces in photos and videos on any website. Everything happens on your computer.

About

How we present ourselves online has shifted towards optimizations. This has led to phenomenons like mewing, biohacking, and procedures to optimize our appearance for reach, trust, or growth. Face Hack hacks every single face that it detects on the feed, satirizing, playing, remixing, or omitting identity altogether from the content that we consume.

Private, Local

Face detection runs on your computer, with a model that ships inside the extension. Your photos are never uploaded and nothing is tracked.
